AR Recurrence Lab

Phase-space trajectories, recurrence plots and recurrence quantification of real market crashes

Each object is the trajectory of a 3D delay embedding x(t), x(t+τ), x(t+2τ) of window-standardised prices (τ = 9, 10 and 5 trading days for the S&P 500, Brent and BTC-USD). Colour runs from light (start of the window) to dark (end of the window). Tap View in AR to place the object on your desk and walk around it.
